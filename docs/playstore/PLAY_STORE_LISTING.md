## Full description

```
Capital BullWave is a market learning and paper trading simulator for Indian markets.

Learn how NSE & BSE markets work using live charts, watchlists, screeners, and educational tools — then practice with virtual money. No real-money deposits or withdrawals.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Paper trading — buy & sell stocks with virtual practice funds
• Live market data — charts, indices, watchlists, news
• Learning hub — calculators, guides, and market education
• Practice wallet — track your simulated portfolio
• Secure account — phone login and identity verification

IMPORTANT
• This app is for learning and simulation only
• Not investment advice
• Not a SEBI-registered broker
• No guaranteed returns
• Real-money investing features are not offered in this version

WHO IS IT FOR
• Students and beginners learning Indian stock markets
• Traders who want to practice strategies without risk
• Anyone exploring markets before opening a real brokerage account

Support: admin@capitalbullwave.com
Privacy: https://capitalbullwave.com/privacy
Terms: https://capitalbullwave.com/terms
```
